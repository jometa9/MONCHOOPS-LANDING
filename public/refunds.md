# Refund Policy

This policy explains when IPTRADE COPIER LLC provides refunds.

---

## 7-day money-back guarantee

We offer a **7-day refund window on first-time paid subscriptions**.

If MonchoOps is not what you expected — for any reason — email **support@monchoops.com** from the email address on your account within 7 days of your first paid charge and we'll refund you in full. No questions, no friction.

---

## Eligibility

You are eligible for a refund if:

- This is your first paid subscription on the account.
- You request the refund within 7 days of the first paid charge.
- The request comes from the account email.

You are **not** eligible if:

- You have already received a refund on a previous subscription on this account.
- You request after the 7-day window.
- You are renewing an existing paid subscription (renewals are not refundable, but you can cancel any time to avoid the next charge).
- Technical issues are on your end (your internet, your proxy provider, your Instagram account being banned for violating Instagram's Terms before, during or after using MonchoOps).
- You used the software in violation of our Terms of Use.

---

## How to request a refund

Email **support@monchoops.com** from the address on your account with:

- Your subscription email.
- The order date.
- A short reason (we genuinely use this to improve the product).

Refunds are processed via Stripe and typically appear in your account within 5-10 business days, depending on your bank.

---

## Cancellations

You can cancel your subscription at any time from your dashboard. Cancellation stops the next renewal — you keep access until the end of the current billing period, after which the app drops to the Free plan limits.

---

## Annual plans

Annual plans are eligible for the same 7-day refund window. After 7 days, annual plans are non-refundable but remain active for the full year you paid for.

---

## Contact

Questions about refunds? Email **support@monchoops.com**.
