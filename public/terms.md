## What MonchoOps does

MonchoOps is a desktop application for Instagram outreach that runs on your own computer:

- Connects one or more Instagram accounts using credentials you provide.
- Optionally routes each account through an HTTP or SOCKS5 proxy you provide.
- Scrapes Instagram usernames from profiles, posts, hashtags or locations using your authenticated session.
- Schedules account warmup activity (feed browsing, story views, hashtag likes, follows).
- Sends cold direct messages from your accounts to lists of usernames you control.
- Stores all sessions, leads and message history locally on your machine in an encrypted SQLite database.

We provide the software and the license server. We do not run any of the automation on your behalf.
