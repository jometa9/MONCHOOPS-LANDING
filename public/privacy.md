# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how IPTRADE COPIER LLC collects, uses, and protects your information.

---

## What we collect

When you create an account on monchoops.com or activate the desktop app, we collect:

- **Account email** — used to authenticate you, send transactional email, and bind your subscription to your machine.
- **Stripe customer ID** — generated by Stripe when you subscribe; we do not store credit card information.
- **License key** — a token that authorizes the desktop app to run.
- **Usage telemetry on the website** — page views, button clicks, and similar through Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel (only on the marketing site).

---

## What we do NOT collect

The desktop app **does not send us**:

- Your Instagram credentials, cookies or session data.
- The proxies you configure.
- The leads you scrape.
- The contents of your DMs or DM history.
- Your message variants.
- Any information about who you message or when.

All of that data lives **only** on your local machine in an encrypted SQLite database. We have no remote access to it.

---

## What the desktop app sends to monchoops.com

To verify your subscription and check for updates, the desktop app contacts our servers to:

- Validate your license key and subscription status.
- Check for new versions of the application.
- Authenticate you when you sign in via email/password or Google OAuth.

Each of these requests includes your authenticated session token. None of them includes Instagram-related data.

---

## How we use your information

- Provide the service (software access, license validation).
- Bill you (via Stripe).
- Send transactional emails (welcome, receipt, password reset, subscription changes).
- Communicate important product updates (security fixes, breaking changes).
- Run the marketing website (analytics, OG previews).

We do **not** sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing.

---

## Service providers

We rely on the following processors:

- **Stripe** — payments.
- **Resend** — transactional email.
- **Vercel / Railway** — hosting.
- **PostgreSQL** — our database (account-level data only).
- **Google Analytics + Meta Pixel** — marketing site analytics, behind a cookie consent prompt.

Each provider has its own privacy policy.

---

## Cookies

The marketing website uses cookies for analytics and ads attribution. The desktop app does **not** use cookies in any web sense — it stores Instagram session cookies locally on your disk for its own use.

See our Cookies Policy for details.

---

## Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR, CCPA, etc.) you can:

- Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- Request correction or deletion of that data.
- Withdraw consent for marketing analytics.
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email **support@monchoops.com** from the address on your account.

---

## Data retention

- Account data: kept while your account is active and for 12 months after you delete it (for billing and tax records).
- Email logs: 12 months.
- Marketing analytics: as configured per provider (typically 14-26 months).
